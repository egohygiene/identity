# Editable outreach examples

These opt-in drafts use the existing fictional
[expanded v1 consumer](../v1/README.md):

- [Email](email.md): recipient, subject, body and campaign placeholders.
- [Social kit](social-kit.md): maintainer, contributor and adopter variants,
  channel suggestions, links, hashtags, visual rights and alt text.
- [Approved fictional visual](../launch/release-announcement/assets/mark/example-expanded-mark.svg):
  the unchanged existing Example mark. No new artwork is generated here.

The reused sentence is exactly its approved public `short` Press Kit
boilerplate. Every campaign-specific sentence and placeholder remains draft
material. All approvals and rights in this consumer are synthetic examples;
they do not authorize a real project’s claims or artwork.

Follow the [end-to-end consumer guide](../../docs/consumers/OUTREACH_KITS.md)
to export approved Press Kit material, copy/edit the drafts and preview them.

Structural inspiration: the [Open Source Pledge resource kit](https://opensourcepledge.com/resources/)
links [recruiting email templates](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1vLlYN640Q-pEBSMinRgOpKLtKenTIbi7jTL_PJErS2k)
and a [social media kit](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1xLNPoy0IYUJykUi-ohHqnxjeU-Xt9POX6VDnE2ws_GU).
The wording here is original example copy; upstream artwork and text are not
included. This does not imply affiliation or endorsement.
