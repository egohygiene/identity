# Kern mascot kit

Kern is Identity's calm, hooded guide. His three warm-white eyes stand for
observing context, reflecting intent, and verifying the resulting projection.
The layered diamond identity kernel is fixed into his chest garment; it is not
a floating prop.

## Choose a variant

| File | Minimum width | Use |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `kern-full.png` | 320 CSS px | Hero art and spacious editorial layouts |
| `kern-portrait.png` | 160 CSS px | Cards, callouts, and square feature panels |
| `kern-icon.png` | 96 CSS px | Compact avatars and small square surfaces |

Use these supplied crops. Do not shrink the full figure below its minimum or
create an ad hoc crop.

## Usage and accessibility

- Keep at least one glowing-eye diameter of clear space around the silhouette.
- Prefer obsidian, charcoal, transparent, or quiet neutral backgrounds.
- Preserve all three eyes, both open hands, and the chest-integrated kernel.
- Pair Kern with visible text. He must never be the sole carrier of a heading,
  label, status, instruction, navigation target, or approval state.
- Use the reviewed alt text from `manifest.json` when Kern adds information.
  Use an empty alt attribute when adjacent content already communicates it.
- Do not recolor, stretch, mirror, rotate, or imply endorsement, sentience, or
  human approval through the character.
- Do not add floating objects, orbit rings, particle trails, extra facial
  features, or detached emblems.

Static presentation is the default and the reduced-motion fallback. Any future
motion needs separate human review and may not introduce orbiting objects,
particles, rapid flashes, or perpetual rotation.
